AI will not just.

July 31, 2026

The new big thing in the AI world this week is to be afraid of Effective Accelerationism (e/acc)1

The funny part is that the decels2 and doomers3 preaching that the end is nigh are exactly the workers of the frontier labs. Something totally organic and of free will.

Ok, it wouldn’t be funny if it weren’t for the timing. And it’s like they are not even trying to hide the trick they are trying to play.

The timing alone tells the story; OpenAI pulled the Hugging Face hack4 (the Anthropic Maneuver5), while Anthropic itself got caught red-handed6 trying to be funny with the US government, a move so clumsy it triggered a harsh open letter from Jensen Huang7 and sparked a new Free AI / open-weights movement8 that united basically every other lab against Anthropic and OpenAI (yeah, totally gratuitous; maybe it was just FOMO9).

Not only that, but now they’re trying to force people to believe that the West needs to slow down the development10 of more advanced AIs, at the first moment in history that Chinese labs are releasing frontier11 or SOTA12 open-weights13 models.

That reminds me of this ludicrous, and very well-known, text famous from the Tumblr (rip) era:

Everyone will not just

https://www.tumblr.com/squareallworthy/163790039847/everyone-will-not-just
If your solution to some problem relies on “If everyone would just...” then you do not have a solution. Everyone is not going to just. At no time in the history of the universe has everyone just, and they’re not going to start now.

This is pure clown-world, live!
And it will obviously not happen.

And the reasons are various14…

China will not just15. Open-source will not just16. Money will not just17. Good researchers will not just18 sit still if another lab has more GPUs and less lecture time. Even the labs doing the preaching will not just19: they keep training, they keep shipping, and “safety” is very useful when it is also a moat. You do not get a global pause20 from a blog post, a bill, or a sad thread, not when the prize for going first is huge and the prize for waiting is looking stupid later21.

So the whole pitch is always the same joke: if we22 would just slow down, if they23 would just listen, if everyone would just stop racing24, the story ends clean. It will not. AI will not just25. Somebody, somewhere, will ship the next model on a random Tuesday because they can26. The rest is costume politics27 with better PR.

You can’t stop the evolution.

The genie is out of the lamp28. The path is straightforward29. You can walk, run, dash, or roll30; it will not stop. The person behind you does not care about your feelings31, and they are probably in a bigger hurry than you32. More chips. More FOMO. More “if we don’t, someone else will”33. You go slow on purpose, and they pass you like you are standing still.

Speed generates more speed34. A better model helps you write better code, clean better data, test faster, and plan the next run while someone else is still writing the “we should pause” thread35. The lab that has the best model this month is not only selling a chatbot; it is using that model to get to next month sooner36. You cannot talk that loop into stopping, because the loop does not need your permission37.

So no, everyone will not just. China will keep shipping. Open weights will keep leaking and landing. Money and talent will keep walking toward whoever is still accelerating38. Every time the “slow down” chorus peaks, something drops anyway: a paper, a leak, a new open model, a new training run39. AI will not just. Speed will not just. And the line of progress40 does not care how pretty your safety slide deck was.

While I was revising this text, Anthropic published a press release41 announcing that Claude had just pulled the exact same stunt42 as the OpenAI model did with Hugging Face.
